01Your cities

Add a city. Then call it whatever you actually call it.

Search 6,312 cities and keep the ones that matter. Every row is honest: exact time, real date, the UTC offset, whether the sun is actually up, and the whole twenty-four hours mapped underneath with night shaded in. Rename any of them — Mom, Dev team, Standup — and the name follows into every widget.

Daylight saving is never your problem. Times come from the IANA timezone database that ships with the OS, so the spring-forward that breaks other apps is just a Sunday here.

02Home-screen widgets

Nine widgets. None of them ever freeze.

Most world-clock widgets redraw the time with background work. Battery optimisation, Doze and OEM task killers shut that work down, and the widget quietly stops at whatever time it last showed. Unichrono runs nothing in the background. The clock ticks from the system clock itself — so it stays live and correct with no process of ours running. Samsung, Xiaomi and Vivo included.

03Time travel & planning

Ask the clock: what if it were six hours later?

Drag the slider and every city answers at once — who's asleep, who's at lunch, whose Tuesday hasn't started yet. The tinted band on each row is someone's 09:00–18:00; when the marker lands inside it, that call is civilised. In the app the range runs a hundred years each way, so you can check a wedding in 2031 or a deadline in 1998.

Found a moment that works? The plan board turns it into a real alarm or a real calendar event, in the right timezone, without arithmetic.

04Alarms, alerts & spoken time

Alarms that don't get jet-lagged.

Anchor a reminder to Lisbon 09:00 and it stays Lisbon 09:00 — even after you land in Osaka and your phone no longer knows which way is up. Unichrono converts it to wherever you actually woke up, repeats on the weekdays you choose, and can notify quietly or say it out loud. The switches below work and the countdowns are live.

Under the list: the time announcer. Let the app call the hour out loud — every hour, or once each morning. On-device voice with a matching haptic, nothing sent anywhere. “Say the time” really speaks.

06What it doesn't do

No account. No network. Nothing to leak.

A clock does not need a server. Unichrono ships the city list and the timezone rules inside the app, so it works on a plane, in a basement, and on the day the vendor's API goes down — because there isn't one.

No trackingNo analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Nothing to opt out of.
Fully offlineCities and timezone rules ship with the app. No API, no sync, no sign-in.
Your data stays putYour cities and settings live on the device. We never see them.
Kind to your batteryZero background work to keep time. The widget ticks from the system clock.
