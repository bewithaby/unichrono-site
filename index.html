06What it doesn't do

No account. No network. Nothing to leak.

A clock does not need a server.

Unichrono ships all cities and every timezone rule inside the app — so it works in airplane mode, in a basement, and on the day other apps stop working because somebody's server went down.

There is no server. There is no account. Your phone already knows the time; Unichrono just reads it.

No trackingNo analytics, no crash SDKs, no ads. Nothing to opt out of.
Fully offlineOn Android the app has no network permission at all. It cannot phone home.
Your data stays putCities, labels, widgets and alerts live on the device and leave with the app.
Kind to your batteryZero background work to keep time. The widgets read the system clock.
