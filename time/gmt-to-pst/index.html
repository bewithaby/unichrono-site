    About these abbreviations

    • GMT: UK time (London). Strictly GMT is UTC+0 all year; the UK moves to BST, UTC+1, in summer, and this page follows the UK clock.
    • PST: US Pacific Time (Los Angeles). In summer it is PDT; people often say PST all year, and this page follows the clock, not the label.
