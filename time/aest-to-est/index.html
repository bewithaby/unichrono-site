AEST to EST

Eastern Time is 16 hours behind Australian Eastern Time in January, when the clocks read AEDT (UTC+11) and EST (UTC−5), and 14 hours behind in July, when they read AEST (UTC+10) and EDT (UTC−4).

    About these abbreviations

    • AEST: Australian Eastern Time (Sydney, Melbourne), AEDT in the southern summer. Brisbane stays on AEST all year.
    • EST: US Eastern Time (New York). In summer it is EDT; people often say EST all year, and this page follows the clock, not the label.
