    About these abbreviations

    • CST: US Central Time (Chicago), CDT in summer. China Standard Time is also written CST; that is a different zone, UTC+8.
    • EST: US Eastern Time (New York). In summer it is EDT; people often say EST all year, and this page follows the clock, not the label.
