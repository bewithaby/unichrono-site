PST to UTC

Coordinated Universal Time is 8 hours ahead of Pacific Time in January, when the clocks read PST (UTC−8) and UTC, and 7 hours ahead in July, when they read PDT (UTC−7) and UTC.

    About these abbreviations

    • PST: US Pacific Time (Los Angeles). In summer it is PDT; people often say PST all year, and this page follows the clock, not the label.
    • UTC: Coordinated Universal Time, the reference every other offset counts from. It never changes.
